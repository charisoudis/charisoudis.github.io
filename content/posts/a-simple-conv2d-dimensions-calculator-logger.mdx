When sketching CNNs, I often need quick feedback on spatial sizes after `Conv2d` (and sometimes `ConvTranspose2d`). Below is a small calculator that:
- computes output height/width using the standard formulae,
- lets you stack layers and see the full shape trace,
- copies the log for PRs or design docs.

> **Formulas (H×W)**
> **Conv2d**: `Ho = floor((Hi + 2p - d*(k-1) - 1)/s + 1)`
> **ConvTranspose2d** (output padding = 0): `Ho = (Hi - 1)*s - 2p + d*(k - 1) + 1`
