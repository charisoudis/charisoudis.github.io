The report analyzes time series from video projections to build representative models and enable forecasting. It examines series 3 (“A”) and 13 (“B”) using exploratory plots (histogram, ACF, PACF), then applies variance stabilization and detrending to achieve stationarity and assesses the resulting stationary series with the corresponding diagrams.
